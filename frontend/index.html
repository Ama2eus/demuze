About

Demuze v2.0 · MIT License

Why Demuze is different

Most stem separators are SaaS — they charge per track, upload your audio and throttle quality on free tiers. Demuze is yours: open-source, local, GPU-accelerated when available, and it never sends a byte to any server.

High Quality mode runs multiple shifted predictions for cleaner separation on demanding material, and the optional noise gate trims low-level bleed after separation.

About

Demuze is a 100% offline, open-source audio stem separator built on Demucs htdemucs_6s by Meta Research. Your audio never leaves your machine.

Privacy

No audio upload, no account, no API key, no subscription, no usage-based limits and no telemetry built into the application. Internet access is only required during installation and model download.
